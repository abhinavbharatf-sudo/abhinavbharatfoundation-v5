The Taluka Agriculture Officer (TAO) serves as the primary technical coordinator for Maharashtra state digital agricultural transformation programs. Key responsibilities include digital crop monitoring, farmer cluster management, e-Pik Pahani digital surveys, and PM-KISAN verification across assigned talukas.

Project Overview & Duration:

Mahatma Phule Krishi Sanjeevani Digital Outreach Project. Direct contract tenure of 11 Months (Renewable based on annual cluster performance review).

Qualifications, Eligibility & Required Documents

Mandatory Eligibility Criteria

  • 4-Year Bachelor's degree in Agriculture (B.Sc. Agri / Horti) from an ICAR recognized SAU.
  • Knowledge of Marathi (speaking, reading, and writing) is mandatory.
  • Age between 21 and 38 years as on 01 January 2026.
  • Valid 2-wheeler or 4-wheeler driving licence with personal vehicle availability for field transit.

Documents Required for Verification (Post-Selection)

Aadhaar Card (Govt Photo ID)
Graduation Degree Certificate / Marksheet
Maharashtra Domicile Certificate

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: What is the application fee for this position?

There is no application fee for the Taluka Agriculture Officer post. Applying is completely free of cost.

Q: Can I apply for multiple positions simultaneously?

Yes. You may apply for multiple positions. Each application maintains its own record under your permanent Master Lead.

Interviews are conducted either via online video panel or in person at the respective District Superintending Agriculture Office.
